⚡ Quick Rules

🎯 Goal: Score more goals than your opponent across 3 periods (10:00 clock, 5 turns each per period).

🎲 Turn: Advance → Draw 6 → Roll & Assign → Shoot (optional) → Buy → End Turn.

⚡ Energy: Spend E faces to buy dice, buy tokens (3E each), or pay costed (⚡) faces.

🏒 Shooting: Commit S-dice + shoot tokens. Defender commits B-dice + block tokens + clock bonus (+1/+2/+3). Roll combat dice — most hits wins.

🛒 Buying: Pay cost + trash an iced rookie, die to Bag. Tokens 3E each (no limit). Sell out a card for a bonus token. Goalies give +1 Block token per buy.

👊 Fighting: Goon with FT can fight an opponent's die (3v2, ties lose). Win: target iced. Lose: penalty die to your bag. Goon ices either way.

🎲 Dice: Rookie E1/E2/S1/B1/blank/blank. Forwards shoot, Defensemen block, Goalies block + shutout (SO2, costed).

⏰ Clock: 10:00→01:00. Red zone 02:00–01:00 only — 10:00–03:00 is cold/white (block bonus +1, +2 at 02:00, +3 at 01:00). Tie after 3 periods → shootout.
